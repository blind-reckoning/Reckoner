We're designing a smart blinds pcb. Its goal is to sit in between the dumb buttons and the motor and:
    1. In offline mode receive the button press and raise all the way up or down the blind. Further button presses will result in halting the movement in the current state.
    2. When used in conjunction with Homeassistant it should expose apis to be operated by automations and whatnot.
Further features may Follow but the hardware should be designed around these considerations.
